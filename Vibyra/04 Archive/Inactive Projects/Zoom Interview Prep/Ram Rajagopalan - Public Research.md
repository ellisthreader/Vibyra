## Public product philosophy

### Start with the customer pain

In a June 2025 CX Today interview, Ram advises teams not to begin by asking where to use agentic AI. Start with the customer-experience pain, then choose the intervention: agent assist for agent-support problems, a virtual agent for high volume and limited availability, or proactive service when upstream signals can prevent contact.

### Skills and prompts replace rigid intent trees

Ram describes a move from scripted intent-based models to skills, prompts and bounded objectives that allow an agent to reason, adapt and act from real-time context. The interview implication is to explain autonomy boundaries, permissions, tool grounding and escalation, not just the happy path.

### Resolution is the objective

Zoom's 17 July 2026 article ties Ram to a high-performing-agent foundation: rigorous pre-launch testing, learning from knowledge gaps and full-context human handoff. The lifecycle continues through shared AI custom skills, unified QA across virtual and human interactions and production refinement. The goal is resolution rather than deflection.

### Evaluation is pre- and post-production infrastructure

Ram's Coval partnership material emphasizes systematic evaluation at scale rather than manual spot checks. Relevant conditions include generated and real-call scenarios, edge cases, accents, background noise, latency, languages, knowledge retrieval, tool calls, goal completion, compliance, regression tests and production monitoring.

### Reuse matters

Zoom's article calls the shared custom-skill pattern "build once, use everywhere": a governed integration can serve both Zoom Virtual Agent and human agents through AI Expert Assist. This links product architecture to faster deployment and lower duplicate maintenance.

## Interview application

- Lead with StyleHub's WISMO pain and explain why ZVA is preferable to agent assist, proactive notifications or process repair for the selected scope.
- Present a versioned golden set plus AI-generated edge cases and plan at least two to three simulation/refinement cycles before launch.
- Define severe-failure gates for fabricated order status, unauthorized action, privacy leakage and broken handoff.
- Show a closed loop: production gap -> reviewed fix -> versioned prompt/knowledge/tool change -> regression case -> controlled release.
- Test voice under noise, accents, language switching, latency and tool/API failure, not only clean transcripts.
- Preserve identity, intent, variables, attempted actions, summary and escalation reason for the human agent.
- Measure the entire resolution: outcome, repeat contact, tool success, handoff quality and virtual/human QA, not containment alone.
- Explain which repeated customer need becomes a reusable capability for the FDE/product roadmap.
