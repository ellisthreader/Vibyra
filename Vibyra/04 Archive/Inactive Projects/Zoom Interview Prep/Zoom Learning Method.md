# Zoom Learning Method

The Zoom CX app is a reference implementation of the reusable [[03 Resources/Interview Preparation/Interview Preparation System|Interview Preparation System]]. Its efficient loop is:

1. Learn a small role-relevant module.
2. Retrieve the answer without notes.
3. Compare against explicit expected signals.
4. Receive a score and one specific next focus.
5. Repeat weak content through confidence-rated flashcards.
6. Practise spoken delivery and architecture drawing under interview-like pressure.

## Current learning surfaces

- **Structured modules:** opening pitch, architecture, API/integration, RAG, voice, reliability, metrics, lifecycle, and behavioural stories.
- **Quizzes:** checks understanding rather than passive reading.
- **Scored interview:** typed or spoken answers evaluated against role-specific signals.
- **Flashcards:** 10-card rounds, 1–5 confidence rating, weak-card prioritisation, checkpoints, mastery, and history.
- **Glossary:** plain-English definitions with interview usage.
- **Whiteboard:** retrieval through drawing and verbal explanation; saved locally and exportable.

## Answer structures

### Technical

**Problem → architecture → trade-offs → reliability → customer impact**

### Behavioural

**Situation → responsibility → action → result → lesson**

## What must change for another interview

- Company, role, stage, interviewer, products, vocabulary, and metrics.
- Questions and their expected signals.
- Model answers and follow-ups.
- Relevant evidence stories and claim boundaries.
- Knowledge modules, quizzes, glossary, and flashcards.
- Scoring rubric and readiness thresholds.
- Branding and local-storage keys, so progress does not mix across interviews.

## What should remain

- Attempt-before-answer interaction.
- Confidence-weighted review.
- Short study rounds with measurable progress.
- Critical scoring tied to explicit signals.
- One clear next improvement after each attempt.
- Spoken practice, follow-up probing, and visual architecture rehearsal.
- Local fallback when model scoring is unavailable.
