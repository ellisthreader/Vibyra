# Gilbert and Rose - Page and Content Inventory

## Global navigation

Primary audiences and divisions: Buyers/Sales, Sellers, Tenants, Landlords, New Homes, Commercial, Business Transfer and Our Team. Utility routes include property search, login, create account, contact, locations, valuation and a Tinder-like property swipe experience.

The homepage is a division gateway with the strapline **“The property marketing professionals”** and entry points for Sales, Lettings, New Homes, Commercial, Business Transfer and external luxury brand Niche Homes.

## Notable content-quality observations

- Some dynamically animated counters were extracted as zero even though supporting copy implies populated values; verify them in a rendered browser.
- Several visible copy errors exist, including “Proffesional”, “Residental”, “TenantManagement”, “PLaecement” and duplicated words such as “covered covered”.
- The site mixes “Southend”, “Southend-on-Sea” and “Leigh-on-Sea” targeting intentionally for local SEO.
