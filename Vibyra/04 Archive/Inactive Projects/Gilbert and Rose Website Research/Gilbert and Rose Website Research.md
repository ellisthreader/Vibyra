## Executive summary

Gilbert & Rose positions itself as an Essex and Southend property-marketing agency spanning residential sales, lettings, new homes, commercial property, business transfer and selected care-home content. The site leads with visual property marketing, instant valuation, property discovery and social proof.

The visual language is distinctive: charcoal, vivid yellow, white and grey; Rubik typography; bold uppercase headings; rounded cards and controls; large photography; yellow calls to action; animated sections; partner-logo strips; property carousels; and location-led SEO pages.

The property catalogue is powered by Rex Software/CDN data. Property pages combine pricing, status, bedrooms, bathrooms, type, description, images, video, floor plan, EPC, map/local information, enquiry actions and related listings. The public sitemap appears to retain many current and historical listing URLs.
