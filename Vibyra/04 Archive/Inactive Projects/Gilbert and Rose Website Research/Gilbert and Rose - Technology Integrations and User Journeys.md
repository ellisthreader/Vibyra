## Main user journeys

### Buyer

Homepage/division entry → property search or location page → filters/listing cards → property details/gallery/floor plan/EPC → book viewing or call → optional account for favourites and alerts.

### Seller

Sales or Sell Your Home → photography/video/drone/360 marketing proposition → trust/review proof → instant valuation platform → lead submission.

### Tenant

Tenants → rental search → property detail/viewing → account/favourite flow. Existing tenants can go directly to Fixflo to report repairs.

### Landlord

Landlords → service and technology explanation → Let Only vs Fully Managed comparison → valuation, WhatsApp or property-search CTA.

### New-home buyer/developer

New Homes → current development listings or land/development proposition → property details/contact.

### Commercial/business client

Commercial or Business Transfer → relevant listings and expertise → commercial/business valuation selector → enquiry.

## SEO structure

The site targets service + location phrases through headings, page titles, FAQs and dedicated area pages. Important themes include estate agents Essex/Southend, houses and flats to rent in Southend, new homes Essex, commercial property Essex, business sales Essex and town-specific “houses for sale” routes.

The XML sitemap is unusually listing-heavy and updates property timestamps frequently. For a rebuild, generate sitemap partitions, canonical URLs and current availability rules rather than allowing indefinitely retained inactive listings to dominate crawl budget.
