The current working tree is dirty. See `Vibyra/_ai/App/Mobile Validation And Delivery.md` for build provenance.

Apple requires App Store privacy answers about data collected by the app **and integrated third-party code**, even when collection is for app functionality. Apple defines collection as transmission off-device with access beyond the real-time request. Optional disclosure has narrow cumulative conditions; a user opt-in alone does not remove an analytics category. App Store answers must cover every platform attached to the same app record and be updated when practices change. [Apple App Privacy Details](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/), [Manage App Privacy](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/manage-app-privacy).

The existing manifest marks each listed type as linked and not used for tracking; this is a conservative source declaration, not verified store metadata. “No tracking” remains conditional on auditing all SDKs, vendors, and actual use under [Apple's tracking definition](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/).

## Minimum final acceptance run

1. Freeze and archive a reproducible commit. Capture final iOS `Info.plist`, entitlements, privacy report, SDK manifests/signatures, binary dependencies, exact production endpoints and build hashes. Resolve all archive/source discrepancies.
2. On an installed signed iPhone build, test fresh guest and new 18+/UK account flows; Terms/Privacy links; account deletion and rights request; camera/QR, photos, speech/microphone, local network, notifications, support screenshot/diagnostics; offline/denied states; free-first wallet and any enabled StoreKit purchase/restore.
3. Capture network traffic and server records for no analytics consent, aggregate consent, linked consent, withdrawal and deletion. Confirm no raw audio or unexpected SDK/Dev Client transmission, and verify each actual third-party recipient and retained category.
4. Reconcile manifest, privacy report, public policy, retention inventory and App Store Connect answers. Resolve the Diagnostics and other open rows explicitly; store screenshots of saved App Privacy, age rating, accessibility, support/privacy URLs, UK-only availability with France excluded, export questionnaire and IAP configuration.
5. Open every public legal/support/right-request URL anonymously from a real device. Submit a test rights request through the production workflow and verify timely owner retrieval, fulfilment and audit trail. Verify a working support channel and the legal entity/contact details before release.

This worksheet is an engineering release control, not a legal certification. Any new data recipient, SDK, paid offer or territory changes the answers and requires another review.
